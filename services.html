What We Offer

Every kind of vehicle move, under one roof

Single Vehicle Delivery

Door-to-door transport for one vehicle. We collect at your address, photo the condition, strap it down securely and deliver to the destination — with updates along the way.

  • Residential & business addresses
  • Run-and-drive or non-runner capable
  • Same-day collections in Gauteng
  • Photo condition report included
🏛️

Dealer Transfers

Reliable inter-dealer stock movement on planned routes. Keep your showroom inventory rotating without tying up your own drivers or risking your vehicles on the road.

  • Planned weekly routes
  • Batch transfers available
  • Proof of delivery & condition reports
  • Volume pricing for regular accounts
🛣️

Long Distance — Durban & Cape Town

Our flagship long-haul corridors: Gauteng ↔ Durban and Gauteng ↔ Cape Town, run on regular departures with tracked, insured loads and direct driver contact.

  • Fixed departure days
  • Live progress updates
  • Coastal & interior routes
  • Insured in transit
🔨

Auction Collections

We collect from auction houses, repossessions and body shops — including non-runners that can't be driven — and deliver straight to your yard or premises.

  • Major auction venue pickups
  • Winning-bid collections
  • Winch load-up for non-runners
  • Same-day Gauteng delivery
🚛

Fleet Relocations

Move an entire fleet between depots, provinces or offices with a single coordinated schedule, one point of contact and volume pricing that makes sense.

  • Volume pricing
  • Staggered or bulk moves
  • Dedicated move coordinator
  • Zero disruption to operations
🛡️

Insured & Tracked Transport

Every load we carry is secured with proper tie-downs and covered in transit, with photographic condition records taken at both collection and delivery.

  • Transit cover options
  • Photo condition reports
  • Direct driver contact
  • Secure strapping every load
